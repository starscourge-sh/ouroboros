# Ouroboros

The ouroboros is an ancient symbol depicting a serpent or dragon consuming its own tail. Derived from Greek, the word literally translates to *"tail-devourer"*.

## Symbolism

**Eternal Cycle** — It embodies the concept of time as an infinite loop: everything that dies is continuously reborn and renewed.

**Unity and Wholeness** — The circular shape represents the paradox of the "All in One", suggesting that beginnings and endings are fundamentally connected.

**Self-Reflection** — In psychology, it represents the human psyche's constant drive for self-awareness, introspection, and personal growth.
